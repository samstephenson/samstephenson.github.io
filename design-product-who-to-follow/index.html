Design & Product:
Who to Follow

A starter follow list for plugging into where tech and product design are heading: people first, then the UK rooms worth being in, then what to read and listen to. It leans toward people who think out loud in public, since that is where the zeitgeist actually shows up.

Product designers and design leaders

The biggest story of 2026 is designers shipping code instead of mocks, and these are the people most visibly working it out in public.
